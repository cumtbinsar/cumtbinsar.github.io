Taiyuan City in northern China has experienced land subsidence exceeding 3 m in some areas due to decades of groundwater overexploitation. Since 2003, water resource substitution, conservation initiatives, and managed aquifer recharge (MAR) strategies have reversed groundwater level decline, leading to surface rebound. This hydraulic head recovery has allowed the aquifer system to complete its consolidation cycle, reaching equilibrium and forming the new preconsolidation head (NPCH). Determining the NPCH aims to address a critical challenge: preventing renewed subsidence in areas that have stabilized. The difference between current hydraulic head and NPCH defines the safe pumping buffer (SPB), a critical groundwater-level threshold for sustainable pumping. This study introduces a framework to characterize the NPCH and SPB in the confined aquifer across Taiyuan City by integrating multi-sensor InSAR-derived deformation with hydraulic head data. Results delineate marked regional variations: northern and central zones show wider SPB ranges, linked to their early initiation of groundwater recovery; eastern and western piedmont zones exhibit narrower SPB due to greater recharge and stable hydraulic conditions; the Wujiabao area displays a delayed subsidence response due to a thick clay interbed within the aquifer system, with the NPCH only being established in 2016. This approach enables the estimation of sustainable groundwater abstraction limits to prevent irreversible compaction while informing adaptive strategies for long-term groundwater management and subsidence mitigation. This study provides a successful example demonstrating that land subsidence induced by groundwater overexploitation can be effectively mitigated through sustained groundwater management.

中国北方太原市由于数十年的地下水超采，部分地区累积地面沉降量已超过 3 m。自 2003 年以来，通过实施水源替代、节水措施和含水层人工补给（Managed Aquifer Recharge，MAR）等策略，地下水位下降趋势得到扭转，并逐渐出现地表回弹。随着地下水水头恢复，含水层系统完成固结周期，达到平衡状态，并形成新预固结水头（New Preconsolidation Head，NPCH）。确定 NPCH 旨在解决一个关键问题：如何防止已经趋于稳定的区域再次发生地面沉降。当前地下水水头与 NPCH 之间的差值定义为安全开采余量（Safe Pumping Buffer，SPB），用于表征在避免再次触发非弹性压密的条件下，地下水水头可允许下降的余量。本研究融合多传感器 InSAR 反演的地表形变与地下水水头数据，建立了表征太原市承压含水层 NPCH 和 SPB 的方法框架。结果揭示了明显的区域差异：北部和中部地区具有较大的 SPB，这与这些区域较早开始地下水恢复有关；东部和西部山前地区由于补给较强、水力条件相对稳定，SPB 较小；吴家堡地区受含水层系统内部厚层黏土夹层影响，表现出滞后的沉降响应，其 NPCH 直到 2016 年才形成。该方法为约束地下水开采、避免不可逆压密提供了依据，并为长期地下水管理和地面沉降防治提供适应性调控思路。本研究展示了一个成功案例，表明地下水超采引起的地面沉降可以通过持续的地下水管理得到有效缓解。
