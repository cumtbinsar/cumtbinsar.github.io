Land subsidence caused by long-term groundwater overextraction has been a persistent hazard in the Qingxu–Jiaocheng area of the Taiyuan Basin, China, while recent management policies have led to substantial aquifer recovery and emerging uplift. To quantify this transition, we integrated L-band ALOS-1 (2007–2010) and C-band Sentinel-1 (2015–2023) data using the SBAS-InSAR technique to construct the spatiotemporal evolution of surface deformation. To separate long-term and seasonal components, we implemented a semi-parametric decomposition framework combining the Locally Weighted Regression (LOESS) method for nonlinear trend extraction with harmonic regression for quantifying annual deformation amplitude and phase. The ALOS-1 results reveal widespread subsidence exceeding 200 mm/yr prior to pumping cessation. In contrast, the Sentinel-1 time series captures a clear transition to surface uplift following groundwater management, with uplift rates reaching +10–25 mm/yr in major recovery zones. Seasonal deformation—concentrated in agricultural zones—exhibits amplitudes up to 25 mm and peaks in February–March, driven by irrigation-induced groundwater drawdown and winter recharge. Spatial offsets between long-term and seasonal deformation distribution highlight the contrasting hydro-mechanical behavior of clay-dominated versus sand-dominated aquifer materials, as well as land-use-driven differences in pumping intensity. Earth fissure distribution aligns with strong deformation gradients and exhibits seasonal modulation linked to groundwater-level fluctuations. Our results demonstrate the value of combining multi-sensor SAR missions with semi-parametric time-series decomposition to monitor groundwater-driven deformation transitions. The findings provide essential insights into aquifer recovery processes, seasonal groundwater dynamics, and associated fissure hazards, supporting sustainable groundwater and hazard mitigation in groundwater-dependent basins.

长期地下水超采引起的地面沉降一直是中国太原盆地清徐—交城地区面临的主要地质灾害，而近年来实施的地下水管理政策推动了含水层恢复，并使部分地区出现地表抬升。为量化这一转变，本研究利用小基线集干涉合成孔径雷达（SBAS-InSAR）技术，整合 L 波段 ALOS-1（2007—2010 年）与 C 波段 Sentinel-1（2015—2023 年）数据，揭示地表形变的时空演化特征。为分离长期与季节性形变分量，研究构建了半参数时序分解框架，结合局部加权回归（LOESS）提取非线性趋势，并通过谐波回归量化年周期形变的振幅和相位。ALOS-1 结果表明，停止抽水前，研究区存在大范围地面沉降，沉降速率超过 200 mm/年；相比之下，Sentinel-1 时序结果揭示了地下水治理后地表由沉降向抬升的明显转变，主要恢复区的抬升速率达到 +10～25 mm/年。季节性形变主要集中于农业区，振幅最高达 25 mm，峰值出现在 2—3 月，与灌溉抽水导致的地下水位下降及冬季补给有关。长期形变与季节性形变分布之间的空间错位，反映了黏土与砂质含水层材料不同的水力—力学响应，以及土地利用差异引起的抽水强度变化。地裂缝分布与较大的形变梯度相对应，并表现出与地下水位波动相关的季节性变化。研究表明，结合多传感器 SAR 数据与半参数时序分解方法，能够有效监测地下水变化驱动的地表形变转变，为认识含水层恢复过程、季节性地下水动态及相关地裂缝灾害提供依据，并为依赖地下水的盆地地区开展可持续地下水管理和灾害防治提供支持。
