2025 年4月27日-5月2日，2025年欧洲地球科学联盟（EGU）年会在奥地利维也纳举行。

唐伟老师参加会议，并在分会场“Integrating Imaging Geodesy and Artificial Intelligence for Natural Hazard Resilience and Disaster Management”做英文口头汇报。

汇报题目为“Production-induced three-dimensional surface displacement over oil/gas fields measured by InSAR and its induced environmental impacts”
